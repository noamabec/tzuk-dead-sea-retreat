−090mGetting here

An hour from Jerusalem, and the last kilometer uphill.

From Ben Gurion Airport: about 90 minutes via Route 1 through Jerusalem, then Route 90 south. A rental car is the easiest way to explore.

From Jerusalem: Route 1 east, Route 90 south to the Metzoke Dragot junction, then the winding road up the cliff. About 60 minutes.

The last kilometer is steep and winding, paved and fine for any car. It's a little scary in the dark, so try to arrive before sunset.

Address: Metzoke Dragot, Dead Sea, Israel

−230mHouse rules

A few things worth knowing.

Cancellation

Free up to 14 days before arrival. Between 14 and 7 days, 50% back. Under 7 days, no refund. Full details in the terms.

Children

Any age at Melach. Sela from age 10, and Shachar is adults only.

Pets

Sorry, no, because of the cliff and the unfenced view side of the pool.

Smoking

Only by the fire pit. Never inside the units or at the pool.

Quiet

The compound is quiet after 23:00. Speaker music stays inside the unit.

Events

No parties or events. Proposals are warmly welcome.

−300mFAQ

What people ask us.

Is the pool heated?
Yes. From November to April the water is around 29°C. In summer it doesn't need heating.
Is there food nearby?
Restaurants at Ein Gedi and the Dead Sea beaches, about 20 minutes away. Every unit has a kitchenette, and you can book a chef's dinner.
Can I come by public transport?
A bus stops at the Metzoke Dragot junction. From there it's 4 km uphill, and we're happy to pick you up if you arrange it ahead.
What's the difference between the units?
Shachar is for couples with a sunrise jacuzzi, Melach for families with a full kitchen, and Sela is stone with a private courtyard. Full comparison.
Is summer too hot?
July and August are very hot at midday. Mornings and evenings are pleasant and the units are air-conditioned. Most guests head out at 7 am.
Is there signal and WiFi?
Good mobile signal and WiFi across the compound. It's weaker at Sela because of the stone walls.
Can I check out late?
If nobody arrives that day, gladly and at no charge. Ask us in the morning.
Is it accessible?
Melach is partly accessible, with adjacent parking and a step-free shower. The path to the pool has three steps. Accessibility statement.
