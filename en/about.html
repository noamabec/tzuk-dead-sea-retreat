−040mThe hosts

Our story.

Maya and Ido, and one place that took six years to build.

2016How we found the cliff

Five thirty in the morning.

We went on a sunrise hike in Nahal Dragot and climbed to the cliff edge with a flask of coffee. When the sun came up over Moab, Ido said "here". It took a year to find the landowners, and another to convince them.

2019We built it ourselves

Stone by stone.

We built most of the walls with a local mason, from limestone quarried ten kilometers away. There is no bare concrete here, the roofs are insulated with gravel, and the shower water feeds the olive trees.

3Units, no more

Why only three.

We could have built eight. But then there would be neighbors, noise from the parking and a queue for the pool. Three is the number where we know every guest by name, and the quiet stays part of the price.
