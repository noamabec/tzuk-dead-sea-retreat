Accessibility statement.

Last updated: October 2026

Website accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this site. It follows WCAG 2.1 level AA and Israeli Standard 5568, and was tested in common browsers on desktop and mobile.

  • Full keyboard navigation, including the booking calendar.
  • Text alternatives for images and a clear heading structure.
  • Checked color contrast and resizable text.
  • An accessibility menu (screen corner) for larger text, high contrast, stopping animations and highlighting links.
  • The site respects your device's "reduce motion" setting.

Compound accessibility

Melach is partly accessible: adjacent parking, a step-free entrance and a step-free shower. The path to the pool has three steps, and Shachar and Sela are not wheelchair accessible.
