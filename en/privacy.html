Privacy policy.

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

Name, phone, email, stay dates and any special requests you give us when booking or in the contact form. Payment details are processed by the payment provider and never stored by us.

Why

To manage your booking, send directions and answer your messages. We won't send you marketing without your explicit consent.

Who we share it with

Only with providers that help us serve you (payments, the booking system, guides you booked). We never sell information.

Cookies

The site uses essential cookies only, plus anonymous traffic measurement.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time at stay@tzuk.co.il.
