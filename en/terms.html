Terms & cancellation.

Last updated: October 2026

1. General

These terms apply to every booking made on the website, by phone or on WhatsApp. Making a booking means accepting them.

2. Booking and payment

A booking is confirmed once you receive confirmation by email or WhatsApp. Payment is by credit card at the time of booking. Prices include VAT for Israeli residents and vary by season, day of the week and holidays.

3. Cancellation policy

  • Up to 14 days before arrival: free cancellation and a full refund.
  • Between 14 and 7 days before arrival: 50% refund.
  • Under 7 days, or no-show: no refund.

If road closures, the security situation or extreme weather prevent arrival, we'll offer another date or a full refund.

4. During your stay

Check-in from 15:00, check-out by 11:00. Guest numbers may not exceed the unit's capacity. Smoking only at the fire pit. Quiet after 23:00.

5. Safety

The compound sits on a cliff edge. Please follow the signs and marked paths and supervise children at all times. The pool has no lifeguard and is used at guests' own risk.

6. Damage

Guests are responsible for damage to the unit or equipment beyond reasonable wear.
