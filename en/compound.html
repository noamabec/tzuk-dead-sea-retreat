The compound.

One pool. The whole sea.

A 14-meter infinity pool whose waterline spills into the Dead Sea below. Heated from November to April, open 07:00 to 22:00.

−110mThe sunrise terrace

The sun rises over Moab.

A shared stone terrace on the eastern edge of the cliff. Mornings come with coffee, evenings with wine, and in between nobody is in a hurry.

−160mFire and stars

At night you see the Milky Way.

No city lights here. The fire pit is ready every evening with wood, blankets and chairs, and across the sea the small lights of Jordan flicker.
